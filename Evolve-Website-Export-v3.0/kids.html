KIDSWEAR LOOKBOOK

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ) for kidswear?

Our MOQ typically starts at 300 pieces per style/color. However, we are flexible and can accommodate smaller test batches for new partners depending on the complexity of the design.

Do you provide samples before bulk production?

Yes, absolutely! We provide pre-production samples (prototypes and fit samples) for your approval before we proceed with the bulk manufacturing to ensure the design, fit, and fabric meet your expectations.

Can you add our brand labels and custom packaging?

Yes, we specialize in private-label manufacturing. We can custom manufacture and attach your brand labels, size tags, care instructions, and even provide custom branded packaging.

What fabric options are available for kidswear?

We source a wide variety of premium fabrics including 100% organic cotton, cotton-poly blends, soft fleece, French terry, durable twill, and hypoallergenic fabrics perfect for children's sensitive skin.

What is the typical delivery time for bulk orders?

Once samples are approved, our standard bulk production turnaround is 4 to 6 weeks depending on the order volume and fabric availability. Shipping times vary based on your location and chosen freight method.
